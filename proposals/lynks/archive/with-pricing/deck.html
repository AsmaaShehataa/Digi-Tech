The order lifecycle

A single order is a chain of transactions, each able to fail alone

DiscoveryCustomer finds a product abroad
QuotePrice promised before true cost is known
ProcurePurchase placed with merchant
ConsolidateParcels matched to orders at hub
ClearInternational leg and customs
DeliverLast mile and reconciliation

Amber stages are where margin and trust are usually lost — decisions made on estimates, or on a human correctly matching one thing to another.
