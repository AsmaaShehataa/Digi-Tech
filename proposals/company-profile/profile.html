03 — What we specialise in

Five capabilities, one delivery team

We do not try to be everything. Our specialty is end-to-end product engineering — from the interface people touch to the infrastructure that keeps it reliable.

Product engineering

Web platforms

Customer-facing applications built for conversion and speed — booking flows, catalogues, checkout, dashboards and account areas — with design handled in-house rather than subcontracted.

Mobile

Applications

Customer and operations apps sharing one API and one design language, so the phone experience and the desktop experience never drift apart as features ship.

Integration

Systems & data

Making separate tools behave as one system: payment providers, carriers, catalogues, accounting and internal databases — with clear ownership of data and failure modes.

Automation

Workflow & AI

Removing repetitive human steps — classification, matching, reconciliation, alerting — where the rules are clear enough to encode and the volume makes it worth doing.

Architecture

Multi-tenancy

One platform serving many isolated tenants — separate brands, markets, franchises or partner accounts on shared infrastructure, with data, permissions, branding and reporting kept cleanly separated. Designed into the schema from day one, because retrofitting tenant isolation later is one of the most expensive rewrites a company can face.

07 — Why Digi-Tech

What clients can count on

Source code, documentation and designs are yours. Delivered into your repository and your infrastructure — no proprietary lock-in, no licence tied to us.

Mainstream stack

Business outcomes first

Interfaces matter, but so do unit economics, operations hours and whether the system still runs when we are gone. We design for all three.
